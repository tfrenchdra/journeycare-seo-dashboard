Hospice and home health are separate licences at co-located JourneyCare branches, and nothing here should be read as a claim about which service line is offered where.

Same clicks, a third fewer impressions, a better click rate

September changed the shape of the problem. Google showed the site 18% less often than in August, average position improved six places to the best month of the year, click-through rate rose by a quarter, and clicks came out level. The footprint got smaller and better qualified at the same time. The month still averages page two, and only the last two days of September closed on page one. Whether this is a ranking gain or Google simply dropping the site from searches it was never going to win is the question this month's data cannot settle on its own.

September is the first complete month in every source, and one source is broken. Google Analytics began recording this site on 7 May 2026, so its May holds 25 days and every Analytics chart here uses a daily rate. Search Console's August covered 1 to 30 August; September is whole. SE Ranking stopped collecting organic positions reliably from 26 September, so section 07 ends on the 25th. Direct sessions are switched off in the charts by default and section 09 explains both.

        What Google shows, and what people click

        Nine months of property totals, then September in full detail. This is the only source here that predates the Analytics install, the only one unaffected by the SE Ranking outage, and the one that identifies which searches the site actually appears for.

        Read the impression line and the position line together. Average position improved every week of September while impressions fell every week. That combination has two possible causes and this data cannot separate them: the site genuinely ranks better, or Google stopped showing it for deep queries it never won, which lifts the average without anything improving. Clicks were flat at 465, 513, 485 and 512 across the four full weeks. August remains a 30 day partial month and is excluded from month over month figures.

        Clicks and impressions, indexed to January

        Impressions run roughly twenty times clicks, so plotting both raw would flatten one of them. Each is converted to a daily rate, which corrects for a 28 day February and a partial August, then indexed to its own January rate. The divergence is the finding: Google is showing the site a good deal less often than it did in January, while clicks have very nearly held.

          Where the site ranks, and why this month's numbers come with a warning

          372 tracked keywords in SE Ranking, checked daily. The collection window runs 21 August to 25 September, because the tool stopped returning organic positions reliably after that date across every Digital Reach project, not just this one.

          SE Ranking stopped collecting organic positions reliably on 26 September. Every date from the 26th on is excluded from this section. On 25 September the tool returned a position for 90 of 372 keywords; by 30 September that was 8. The same break happened on the same day across five unrelated domains and four different clients, map pack collection kept working while organic collection failed, and Ahrefs shows this site flat across the same window. It is a tool fault, not a ranking event. The decline visible before the 26th cannot be cleanly separated from the start of that fault either, so treat every count below as a floor rather than a measurement, and use the Search Console figures in section 06 for anything that has to be defended.

              Local pack presence, by town

              This corrects a figure in the previous version of this dashboard. The August build reported that JourneyCare appeared in all 51 map packs it tracked. That 51 was the number of searches the SE Ranking API happened to flag as having a local pack, not the real number. The full export shows 250 of the 372 tracked searches return a map pack, and JourneyCare appears in 47 of them. The win is real and it is narrower than previously stated.

              One further limit. The SE Ranking project still has no search location set, so every geo-modified keyword is checked from a national default. Google builds a map pack from where the searcher is standing, so a recorded appearance is solid and a missing one is unmeasured. Setting the search location per market is the first fix once collection is working again.
