JourneyCare search visibility report. Prepared by Digital Reach Agency, 4 September 2026. Covers journeycare.org only. Sources: Google Analytics 4 (property journeycare.org), Google Search Console (journeycare.org), SE Ranking (project JourneyCare - SEO, 372 tracked keywords) and Ahrefs Site Explorer (United States, subdomains). Every figure carries its tool. Figures from different tools measure different things and are not comparable to one another. Hospice and home health are separate licences at co-located JourneyCare branches, and nothing here should be read as a claim about which service line is offered where.

You are seen constantly and chosen rarely

Google showed journeycare.org roughly 39,700 times in August. Almost all of that was for non-branded hospice searches, and almost none of it turned into a visit, because the site sits on page two. That single gap explains more than any other number here, and it is what the service-area pages exist to close.

Two sources, two windows, and neither end month is whole. Google Analytics only began recording this site on 7 May 2026, so its May holds 25 days. Search Console reaches back to January but its August covers 1 to 30 August and reports with a lag. Every chart here uses a daily rate for that reason, and no month over month figure is calculated against either partial month. Direct sessions are switched off in the charts by default and section 09 explains why.

          What Google shows, and what people click

          Eight months of property totals, then one month in full detail. This is the only source here that predates the Analytics install, and it is the one that identifies which searches the site appears for.

          August is a partial month twice over. It covers 1 to 30 August rather than a full 31 days, and Search Console reports with a lag, so 30 August recorded 5 clicks against a normal daily range of roughly 45 to 120. Treat August as about 29 days of settled data. It is charted and tabled, and it is deliberately left out of every month over month calculation.

          Clicks and impressions, indexed to January

          Impressions run roughly twenty times clicks, so plotting both raw would flatten one of them. Each is converted to a daily rate, which corrects for a 28 day February and a partial August, then indexed to its own January rate. The divergence is the finding: Google is showing the site a good deal less often than it did in January, while clicks have very nearly held.

            Where the site actually ranks, keyword by keyword

            372 tracked keywords, Google United States, checked daily in SE Ranking. Search Console says the site sits around position 19 on average across everything it appears for. This tells you which keywords those are, and the average hides two very different halves.

            Rank tracking started 24 August 2026. This is a snapshot, not a trend. Eight daily checks exist, and 102 of the 372 keywords were added part way through that window, so day to day movement here is setup and normal SERP noise rather than performance. The first comparison worth making comes after the first wave of service-area pages ships.

            Where the tracked keywords sit

            Every tracked keyword placed in a position band, split by whether the keyword contains the brand name. Tracking depth is 100, so "not in top 100" means the site does not appear at all for that term.

              Local pack presence, by town

              Grey is how many keywords are tracked for each town. Blue is how many put JourneyCare in the top 10 organic results. Orange is how many put JourneyCare in the Google map pack. Every map pack appearance the tracker has recorded sits in Barrington, Deerfield or Crystal Lake, and in most of them JourneyCare holds map position one.

              Read the orange bars in one direction only. Where JourneyCare appears in a map pack, that is real and verified. Where it does not, this tracker cannot yet tell you why, because the SE Ranking project has no search location configured and is checking every keyword from a national default. A map pack is assembled from the searcher's location, so a national check under-reports them. Treat "none" here as "not yet measured for this town," not as "absent from the map pack." Setting the search location per market is the fix, and it is the first thing to do before the next pull.
